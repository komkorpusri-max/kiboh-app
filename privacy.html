Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Kiboh is a desktop scheduler for TikTok creators. Each creator runs it on their own computer and connects their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app touches and what it does with it.

1. Data accessed through TikTok Login

When a creator signs in with TikTok, the app requests only these scopes:

  • user.info.basic — the account identifier, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload / video.publish — to upload and publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own TikTok profile.

No email, phone number, password, or private messages are requested or accessed.

2. Where the data is stored

Access tokens and posting records are stored locally on the creator's own computer, in a local database. Nothing is sent to any server controlled by the author, and no analytics, advertising SDK, or tracking is included.

3. Data of other people

The app does not collect, store, or process personal data of any other person. It only operates on the connected account and on the videos the creator chooses to publish.

4. Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties. The only network requests made are to the official APIs of the platforms the creator chooses to connect, primarily TikTok.

5. Content review before publishing

Every video is shown to the creator for review and must be approved before it is published. The app does not publish content on its own without this approval step.

6. Deletion

The creator can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the application's local database file, and can revoke the app's access from their TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage app access).

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact link on the author's public profile, or through the support channel listed on the app's page.
