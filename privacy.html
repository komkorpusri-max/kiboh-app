Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Kiboh is a personal desktop utility. This policy explains what data it touches and what it does with it.

1. Data collected through TikTok Login

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app requests only these basic scopes:

  • user.info.basic — account identifier, display name and avatar.
  • video.upload / video.publish — to upload and post videos to the owner's own account.

No email, phone number, password, or private messages are requested or accessed.

2. Where the data is stored

Access tokens and posting records are stored locally on the owner's own Windows computer, in a local database. Nothing is sent to any server controlled by the author, and no analytics, advertising SDK, or tracking is included.

3. Data of other people

The app does not collect, store, or process personal data of any other person.

4. Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties. The only network requests made are to the official APIs of the platforms the owner chooses to use.

5. Deletion

The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the application's local database file, and can revoke the app's access from their TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage app access).

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact link on the owner's TikTok profile.
