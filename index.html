Kiboh

Kiboh is a small personal desktop utility for Windows. It runs on my own computer and helps me keep a regular posting schedule: it collects video content I have rights to use, prepares short clips, and lets me review everything before anything is posted.

What it does

  • Runs on my own Windows PC on a schedule I control.
  • I review and approve every single post before it is published.
  • It uses TikTok Login only to sign in as me and post to my own account.

What it does NOT do

  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking of other people.
  • No access to anyone else's account or content.
  • No automated behaviour towards other users (no follows, comments, or messages).
