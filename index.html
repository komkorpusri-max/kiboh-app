Kiboh

Kiboh is a desktop scheduler for Windows that helps TikTok creators plan, review and publish their own short videos. It runs on the creator's own computer, connects to the creator's own TikTok account, and keeps uploads on a steady schedule.

Who it is for

Kiboh is for any TikTok creator who wants to prepare and schedule their videos ahead of time instead of uploading manually. Each creator signs in with their own TikTok account and manages only the content they own or have the right to use.

What it does

  • Signs in with TikTok Login so a creator can connect their own account.
  • Helps schedule and upload the creator's own videos to their TikTok profile.
  • Shows every post for review, so the creator approves each video before it is published.
  • Runs locally on the creator's Windows computer, under the creator's control.

What it does NOT do

  • No ads, no selling of personal data, no tracking of other people.
  • No access to accounts other than the one the creator signs in with.
  • No automated behaviour towards other users (no follows, comments, or messages).
  • It never posts anything without the creator reviewing and approving it first.
